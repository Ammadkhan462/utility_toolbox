## Full description
```
Scan documents to PDF, read any QR code, sign and edit PDFs, remove photo backgrounds, copy text from photos and translate offline — 45 tools in one small, fast app.

Utility Toolbox works where you already are: share a photo or PDF to it from any app, select text anywhere and tap Translate, or long-press the icon to jump straight to the scanner.

📄 SCAN & PDF
• Document Scanner – automatic edge detection, multi-page, clean PDF in seconds
• Image to PDF – turn photos into one PDF
• Sign PDF – draw your signature once and place it on any page
• Organize PDF – reorder, rotate and delete pages
• Watermark & Page Numbers – stamp text or number every page
• Merge PDF, Compress PDF, Split PDF (extract or remove pages)
• PDF to JPG and PDF to Text – save pages as images or copy their text
• Lock PDF with a password, Unlock PDF you have the password for
• Word to PDF – convert .docx files

🔳 QR & BARCODE
• Scan QR codes and barcodes from the camera or from a photo
• Create QR codes for links, text and contacts
• WiFi QR – share your WiFi without typing the password
• Quick Settings tile for one-tap scanning

🖼️ PHOTOS
• Remove Background – cut out the subject and save a transparent PNG, or put it on a white background for passport and ID photos
• Compress photos to a smaller file size
• Resize to exact pixels and convert JPG, PNG and WEBP
• Photo editor with filters, crop and adjustments
• AI Upscaler – enlarge photos up to 4×

🔤 TEXT & TRANSLATE
• Image to Text (OCR) – copy text from any photo, now also in Hindi, Chinese, Japanese and Korean
• Offline translator with voice input and speech
• Translate from any app's text-selection menu
• Word counter, case converter, Base64
• Clipboard manager

🧮 CALCULATE & CONVERT
• Currency Converter – 150+ currencies, works offline with the last saved rates
• Percentage & Discount – sale prices, tip split and GST/VAT
• Calculator, Age & Date calculator, Loan & EMI calculator, BMI calculator
• Unit converter, World clock with home-screen widget, Color picker

🔦 EVERYDAY TOOLS
• Flashlight with SOS, strobe and screen light
• Bubble Level – check that a shelf or frame is straight
• Stopwatch with laps and countdown Timer
• Direct Chat – open a WhatsApp chat with any number without saving it to your contacts
• Compass, Save location

📶 MORE
• Internet speed test, IP & ping
• Password generator, Hash generator
• Video to MP3, GIF and MP4

WHY UTILITY TOOLBOX
✔ Fast: opens straight to your tools, with search, favorites and recent tools
✔ Offline: scanning, PDF tools, OCR and translation run on your phone
✔ Private: your files are never uploaded unless you turn on a cloud option yourself
✔ My Files: find, open and share everything you created
✔ Every tool in your language: 12 languages, light and dark theme

Free with ads. Remove ads with a one-time purchase — no subscription.

Privacy policy: https://github.com/Ammadkhan462/utility_toolbox/blob/master/PRIVACY_POLICY.md
```
